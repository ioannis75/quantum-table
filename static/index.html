Three-party agreement the house as third party: three-qutrit singlet (Fitzi, Gisin, Maurer 2001), step 1

    
A qutrit is a 3-sided die (2 qubits, fourth level empty). The singlet makes all three parties see three different values in any common basis, so one liar's report is inconsistent. Illustration of detectable agreement on the Aer emulator, not a contract-signing system; the house still sees everything classically.
