How a roll works

(1) The house die is prepared as a uniform superposition over faces 1–6 on 3 qubits; your die is a CNOT copy of it (entangled circuit) or an independent uniform die, chosen per roll by the level above (the fraction of rolls that use the entangled circuit, drawn by the emulator's seeded RNG). (2) The circuit is transpiled to your physical qubits and run once through the noise model of the fake backend's calibration; the fidelity shown is the product of (1 − error) over every gate and readout it used. (3) The dice animate to the measured faces. A reading of level 7 or 8 is a misfire: no result. One shot cannot be chi-square tested, so the verifier checks the exact circuit distribution on every roll and runs chi-square and TVD on the aggregate of all rolls with the same settings.
